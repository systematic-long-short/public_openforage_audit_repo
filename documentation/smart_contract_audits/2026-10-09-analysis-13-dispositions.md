## Bounded queue source trace

With `maxEntries=1`, the standard scan limit is 65 candidate units. The source merges the standard array and demoted min-heap by queue ID, persists the cursor and snapshot across calls, defers oversized demoted IDs, and restores deferred IDs after the snapshot ends. Priority demotion keeps the same ID and does not rewind the cursor, snapshot, or restore state. The trace is conditional on priority work completing and no earlier standard candidate returning STOP. It is source reasoning, not runtime or gas evidence.
