## Policy boundaries
Only fresh deployments are supported. New code must refuse pre-fresh state before changing it. No legacy migration engine is supported. In the Blocklist, the legacy importer and interval-translation path are removed; fresh initialization sets the layout version and every state-changing entrypoint checks it before effects. The historical checkpoint lookup uses `wasBlockedAt`; the retained pre-checkpoint mapping is inert and remains only for layout. An independent source review accepts this Blocklist repair within a source-only bound; it proves no old-proxy or deployed-state behavior. The first upgrade still uses the authorizer in the implementation already installed. These rules do not prove every deployed proxy or upgrade path.
Profit belongs to the holders at recognition. Unpaid profit stays a separate claim and is paid only when cash arrives. Withdrawals use cash-backed share value and available cash; they promise no payment date. A reported custodian loss belongs to holders at report time. Transfers and exits that could avoid that loss stay frozen through settlement.
The distributor is the trusted payer, not the recipient. Payment grants no system-account status or restricted-call permission.
Since the 2026-10-28 update, a tier deposit waits while a fresh profit attestation is not yet recognised, and any recognition, including zero, reopens admissions. Each queue entry is at least 100 RISKUSD, and a depositor holds at most 8 open entries. A holder whose allowlist approval lapsed may cancel its own queue entry and collect funded profit to an allowlisted recipient. Neither Treasury is guardian-pausable; both are on the guardian emergency target list so the reserved guardian can tighten their caps, and a pause call on either fails. Since the 2026-10-30 update, a new positive profit recognition waits until earlier recognised profit has come back and been delivered, and a loss is not recognised while reconciled cash waits in the bridge. With no principal deployed, returned profit is not held back by NAV headroom, and the zero-principal sweep refuses cash that recognised profit still claims and waits until the latest fresh NAV report has been answered. Since the 2026-10-31 update, an expired opted-out tier position reverts to Tier 0 before it can withdraw, no position can opt out while a withdrawal is pending, and expiry processing renews an auto-renew position even while it waits to withdraw, so shares the exit returns stay under the new lock. The token's Allowlist is not changed while a vote-sync task is queued. Since the 2026-10-31 round-9 update, a loss on recognised profit that has not come back reduces the holders' unpaid claim only by their share of the profit lost, so holders, agents and the protocol each bear their share of it. A Blocklist block stops an account's FORAGE reward claims; a round that expires during the block is not reopened, and an unblocked account's entitlement may be published again in a later round. The Allowlist gates entry and exit: deposits, queue joins, withdrawals, redemptions and the profit recipient; tier shares and RISKUSD move between any accounts that are not blocked.
